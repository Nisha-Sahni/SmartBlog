Account & Security

To create an account, click the Get Started button on the home page or navigate to signup.html. Enter your full name, email address, and a secure password. Once submitted, your account is created immediately and you will be guided to your personalized dashboard.

You can log in from login.html with your registered email and password. To log out securely, click your profile avatar in the upper right corner of any page (or the logout link in the sidebar) and select Log Out. Confirm the prompt, and SmartBlog will clear all authentication tokens and safely sign you out.

Visit My Profile from the navigation bar. You can view your author statistics, published stories, reputation points, and community standing. To adjust account settings or update your password, open Settings from the profile menu.

Click your profile avatar and choose Settings. From the settings panel, you can review your registered email, manage notification options, review active sessions, and access security controls.

Creating & Managing Content

Click Create a story in the top navigation. Provide an engaging title, an introductory subtitle, select an appropriate category/topic (such as Technology, Lifestyle, or Programming), and choose a visual layout template. Write your content in the editor and click Publish story. Your story is instantly available for others to read.

Navigate to My Profile to view the stories you have written. Click on any of your articles and choose the edit action to update the text, subtitle, or cover image. When you save your changes, the backend immediately updates your blog and re-computes its semantic representation.

Search & AI Recommendations

Go to Search from the top navigation. Type keywords, concepts, or full natural-language questions (e.g., "How to build better morning habits?") into the search bar. You can also filter results by topics such as Technology, Health, Business, or Programming.

SmartBlog uses a lightweight pre-trained sentence embedding model (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) to understand the semantic meaning of your search queries. Instead of strictly matching exact words, it converts both queries and blog contents into 384-dimensional vector embeddings and calculates cosine similarity. This allows you to find relevant stories even if they use completely different wording.

SmartBlog constructs a personalized user preference profile based on your selected interests, reading history, and saved bookmarks. Using content-based NLP filtering, it compares your profile against all available stories to recommend content tailored specifically to your taste. Stories matching your preferred topics receive a relevance boost, and unread stories are prioritized.

Personalization, Reading & Community

Whenever you see a story card on the Dashboard, Explore, or Search page, click the ♡ Save button in the bottom right corner. The button will change to ♥ Saved and the story will be saved to your reading list.

Whenever you open and read a blog story while logged in, SmartBlog securely records the visit in your reading history. You can pick up right where you left off from the Your reading history section on your dashboard, and your reading activity helps refine your personalized recommendations.

You can engage with authors by writing comments at the bottom of any blog post. Additionally, you can visit the Community Q&A hub to ask questions, post helpful answers, discuss ideas with other readers, and earn author reputation points.
